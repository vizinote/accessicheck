What we test — and what we don't

A tool can't see everything. We state our limits right on the homepage, as an honest approach requires.

Automated technical detection

  • Text readable on any background (colour contrast)
  • Well-structured page headings (h1 → h6)
  • Clear page structure for screen readers (ARIA landmarks)
  • Images with a text description (alt attribute)
  • Understandable forms (labels and accessible names)
  • Links whose label makes sense out of context
  • Page language correctly declared
  • Keyboard navigation usable end-to-end (skip-link, visible focus)
  • Buttons and links large enough on mobile (min. 44 px)
  • Videos, PDFs and iframes correctly identified
  • Presence of an accessibility statement (WCAG/EAA requirement)

AI analysis (beyond the classic 40%)

  • Relevance of alternative texts (generic "image", "IMG_1234", filename…)
  • Clarity of link labels ("click here", "read more"…)
  • Clarity of form labels and error messages

The AI analysis flags suspicious semantic points. Its verdicts remain leads to be confirmed by an expert: the tool never claims 100%.

What requires a human audit

  • Real screen-reader compatibility
  • Exhaustive keyboard navigation across all pages
  • Multi-page audit (outside the Pro/Monitoring offers)
  • Overall navigation and comprehension
  • Final validation of full WCAG compliance

No WCAG guarantee. No magic.

AccessiCheck is an express diagnostic

It detects technical issues that are automatically visible and gives you a list of priority actions. It's a first step, not a certification.

Why it's useful anyway

Most sites have dozens of simple technical errors to fix. Identifying them quickly saves you time and money before a possible full audit.

False positives are possible

Like any scanner, AccessiCheck may flag items that aren't real violations. The report states the confidence level each time.

Frequently asked questions

How much time and money will I save?

A professional accessibility audit costs on average €1,500 to €5,500 and takes several days. AccessiCheck doesn't replace it: in 2 minutes (free scan) or within 24 business hours (paid report) it gives you the list of automatically-detectable technical errors, ranked by severity. You fix the most urgent first — and you only commit to a more expensive human audit if your situation really requires it.

Does AccessiCheck guarantee WCAG compliance?

No. AccessiCheck combines automated technical detection with semantic AI analysis (alternative texts, link labels, form labels). It covers a significant but non-exhaustive part of the WCAG criteria. A human audit remains necessary to guarantee full and complete compliance.

Does the scan cover my whole site?

The One-Shot offer analyzes the homepage. The Pro Report covers 5 key pages. Monthly monitoring returns to the homepage each month. For a complete audit of the entire site, you need a human WCAG auditor.

How do I receive the report?

By email, within 24 business hours after payment. The report includes a readable PDF and a list of priority actions.

What payment methods are available?

Payments are secured by Stripe (credit card). Delivery is automatic: you receive the report by email within 24 business hours.

Who is behind AccessiCheck?

AccessiCheck is a product of the Brozapi studio, built and maintained by supervised AI agents. The goal is to make web accessibility understandable for small sites.

Can I get a refund?

If the scan has not yet been launched, contact us for a refund within 14 days. Once the report has been generated, the service is considered delivered.

Aren't accessiBe-style overlay tools enough?

No. Overlays are discredited by the accessibility community (Overlay Fact Sheet signed by over 1,000 experts). They often create new problems and don't replace a real audit. Our "no-magic report" approach is more legally and ethically defensible.
